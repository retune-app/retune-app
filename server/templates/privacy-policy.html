Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 2026

Welcome to Retuned. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience using our app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

Our Promise

We collect only what's needed to power your experience: account info, affirmations, voice samples, wellness session data, and usage stats. Mood check-in data is ephemeral and not permanently stored. We never sell your personal data.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Name (optional, or provided through social login)
  • Authentication tokens for secure access

Voice Samples

If you choose to use our voice cloning feature, we collect:

  • Audio recordings you provide for voice cloning
  • The cloned voice model created from your sample

Your voice samples are processed through ElevenLabs, our trusted voice synthesis partner, solely for the purpose of creating your personalized affirmation audio. Original recordings are deleted immediately after cloning is complete. Stock AI voices within the app are powered by Hume AI, providing expressive voice synthesis with word-level timing.

Affirmations and Content

  • Goals and intentions you enter
  • AI-generated affirmation scripts
  • Custom categories you create

Mood & Wellness Data

To provide personalized wellness experiences, we collect:

  • Mood check-in responses Ephemeral
  • Breathing exercise session data (technique, duration)
  • Micro-meditation session data
  • Journey completion data (mood paths, step completion rates)
  • Ambient sound preferences

Mood check-in responses are ephemeral and are not permanently stored. They are used in real-time to personalize your experience and then discarded.

Usage Data

  • Listening session duration and frequency
  • Listening streaks and category breakdowns
  • Breathing exercise sessions
  • Daily greeting interactions
  • Notification preferences
  • Device push notification tokens (for delivering push notifications to your device)
  • Feature usage patterns
  • App performance data

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and personalize your affirmation experience
  • Generate AI-powered affirmation scripts tailored to your goals
  • Create your personal voice clone for audio playback
  • Provide AI-powered mood check-ins and personalized wellness paths
  • Generate micro-meditations tailored to your current mood
  • Deliver personalized daily greetings based on your activity
  • Track breathing exercise progress
  • Recommend ambient soundscapes
  • Track your progress and streaks
  • Improve our app and services
  • Send you notifications (if enabled), including push notifications about your voice clone status (e.g., expiry warnings)
  • Respond to support requests

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to power your experience:

OpenAI

For generating personalized affirmation scripts, micro-meditation scripts, daily greetings, mood analysis, and breathing wisdom tips.

ElevenLabs

For voice cloning and personalized text-to-speech synthesis of your affirmations.

Hume AI

For stock AI voice synthesis with expressive delivery and word-level timing precision.

Apple Sign In / Google Sign In

For secure, streamlined authentication and account creation.

Each service has its own privacy policy and data handling practices. We only share the minimum data necessary for these services to function.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS)
  • Secure password hashing
  • Session-based authentication for web access
  • Token-based authentication for mobile devices
  • Explicit voice consent required before any voice cloning
  • Regular security updates

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Your personal data is permanently deleted
  • Voice samples and cloned voices are removed
  • Affirmations and listening history are erased
  • Push notification tokens are removed

Push notification tokens are also automatically removed if your device is no longer registered with the notification service.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data (GDPR-compliant)
  • Portability: Export your affirmations and data

To exercise these rights, use the settings within the app or contact us directly. We process all data deletion requests in compliance with GDPR and applicable data protection regulations.

7. Children's Privacy

Retuned is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes through the app or via email.
